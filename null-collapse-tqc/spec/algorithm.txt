Multi-Pair Null Collapse with Amplitude Concentration
======================================================

1. Encode candidate weights w_k via Ry(2 arcsin(w_k)) on qubit k.
2. Optional concentration rounds:
   a. Local concentration: apply sigma_1^2 = Z on each qubit.
   b. Pairwise reflection: braid sigma_even between adjacent pairs.
3. Measure all qubits in computational basis; Zero outcome = null.

Known limits (as supplied):
- Local Z alone does not increase null probability.
- Clifford-only Ising braiding cannot implement general Grover
  amplification without non-Clifford resources.
